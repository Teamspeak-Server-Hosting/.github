## Starting a TeamSpeak Session

Begin by identifying whether you need the TeamSpeak client or a server environment. Review the current product documentation before selecting software or configuring a hosted service.

1. **Confirm Product Identity:** Verify the TeamSpeak product and component appropriate for your communication needs.
2. **Review Requirements:** Check client or server compatibility, network needs, and resource expectations.
3. **Choose a Hosting Approach:** Decide whether a provider-managed service or a self-managed environment better fits your requirements.
4. **Configure the Environment:** Set up channels, permissions, and administrative access according to your intended use.
5. **Test Connectivity:** Confirm that authorized users can connect and communicate as expected.
6. **Review Maintenance:** Document configuration details and establish a process for managing access and updates.

**Communication safety note:** Share server access details only with intended participants, use appropriate permissions, and protect administrative credentials. Respect the rights of other participants and follow the service terms applicable to your environment.
